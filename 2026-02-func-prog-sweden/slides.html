++++ ### We idolize technicalities The more often something is mentioned in: - conference talks - documentation - library marketing …the more value we assign to it. ++++ ## Then reality hits
  • These things don’t matter (directly) for the business
  • They matter indirectly, through a long chain of proxies
  • What matters: how quickly and cheaply you can solve real problems
    • ++++ The dev builds a perfectly safe, performant, maintainable solution... - That’s decommissioned within months - That no one uses - That solves the wrong problem ++++ The opposite extreme is also bad Looking at you, JavaScript ++++ But that’s less common. Because most projects fail before hitting technical issues. ---- Shifting the mindset ## How can we do better? ++++
